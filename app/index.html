Measurement-Induced Phase Transitions (MIPT) in Monitored Quantum Circuits

We investigate the entanglement dynamics of a one-dimensional quantum circuit subjected to projective measurements. Through extensive numerical simulations and analytical mapping to statistical mechanics models, we identify a sharp transition between volume-law and area-law entangled phases, characterized by conformal field theory scaling at the critical point.
